Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains what Trap Devil ("the game", "we") does with your information. The short version: we do not collect your personal information.

Information we collect

The game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, or any account. We do not collect, sell, or share personal information ourselves.

Data stored on your device

Your level progress, death counts, and settings (sound, screen shake, touch controls) are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. You can delete them at any time with Reset progress in the game's Settings, or by clearing the app's data.

Ads

The app version of the game may show ads provided by Google AdMob. This includes optional ads you choose to watch to skip a level. Google may collect and use device information, such as your advertising ID and IP address, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. How Google handles this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset your advertising ID or limit ad personalization in your device's settings. On Android, open Settings, then Privacy, then Ads.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
